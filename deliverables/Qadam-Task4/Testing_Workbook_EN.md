## 2. Participant consent script

Thank you for helping us evaluate Qadam Delivery for a university project. We are testing the product, not your ability. We would like to record your screen and voice while you use staged delivery information. The full recording will be shared with the course instructor through an access-controlled folder, and a short clip may be shown in our final classroom presentation. Please avoid displaying private messages, real customer information or payment details. You may skip a question or stop at any time. Participation has no charge and creates no real delivery. Do you agree to participate, to this recording, and to this classroom/instructor sharing?

Record each permission separately. Obtain additional permission before any wider publication. Do not record before consent. Agree on a retention/deletion date with the participant and team, and record it in the consent record.

## 3. Facilitator script and tasks

Session target: 10-12 minutes; mandatory full recording: at least 3 minutes.

1. Confirm consent, device/build details and that no private information is visible.
2. Say: "Please say what you are looking for and what you expect to happen. I will mostly observe."
3. Present each task card without pointing to a button. The Russian-language interface may remain Russian.
4. Record help as assistance; do not count an assisted task as unassisted success.
5. End each task with the seven-point ease question: 1 = very difficult, 7 = very easy.
6. Debrief: "What was hardest? What would stop you from using this for your next delivery?"

| Task | Neutral participant prompt | Unassisted acceptance criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 - First order | A buyer needs a parcel delivered today. Arrange it in Qadam using this task card. | Correct pickup, destination, recipient and parcel; reviewed and submitted once; <=150 s. Target >=9/10. |
| T2 - Recover and explain | The buyer asks where the parcel is. Reopen Qadam, find the order and explain what you can tell them. | Same order recovered: >=9/10; status and old GPS correctly interpreted: >=8/10; <=90 s. |
| T3 - Repeat and correct | Send a similar parcel to another recipient. Reuse a completed order, correct the details and check the new order. | New ID; intended changed recipient/address; prior order unchanged; <=90 s. Target >=9/10. |

Use the same task card fields for each comparable scenario: test order ID, staged addresses, recipient alias/contact, parcel, selected payment mode and expected result. Keep actual payment separate from a delivery status. No real money changes hands in usability sessions.

## 8. Separate quantified-value time study

This study is proposed, not completed. Use silent timing without think-aloud.

- Sample: five eligible shops, ten matched scenario pairs per shop = 50 pairs / 100 runs. Counterbalance five manual-first and five Qadam-first pairs per shop.
- Same parcel details, agreed price and scripted acceptance/status/completion events for both workflows. Give equal practice and pin the build.
- Sum all active staff work on preparation, acceptance checks, progress retrieval and completion lookup. Include negotiation/corrections/retries when needed. Record passive waits and courier travel separately.
- Cap active run time at a predefined ten minutes; keep capped failures in totals and report them separately. Do not drop slow or failed runs.
- Primary ratio = total manual active seconds / total Qadam active seconds. Also show per-shop ratios, median paired ratios, range and completion counts.
- Target: ratio >=5 and Qadam scenario completion >=90%. The 600/90=6.67x report model is not a measured result.
- A later field pilot is a separate proposed activity, subject to owner approval; it is not automatically authorized or started by preparing this academic report.
